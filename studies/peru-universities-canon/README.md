# How Peru's public universities spend their canon

Peru's canon returns part of the state's revenue from mining, gas and oil to
the regions where those resources are extracted. Public universities in those
regions receive a share. [Law 28077](https://leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/28077.pdf),
passed in 2003, amended the [canon law](https://leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/27506.pdf)
so that each regional government passes a fifth of its canon, 5% of the
total, to the public universities of its region, for scientific and
technological research. The [royalty law](https://leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/28258.pdf)
of 2004 gave them 5% of mining royalties for the same purpose. The budget
law for 2009, [Law 29289](https://leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/29289.pdf),
preferred applied research in public health, farm health, biodiversity,
renewable energy and production. It let universities spend up to 30% on
investment projects tied to their aims and barred paying salaries from the
canon.

For a decade the press has reported that universities leave most of it
unspent. [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/archivo/2014/01/02/universidades-solo-utilizaron-un-14-de-los-fondos-de-canon-que-recibieron-para-investigacion/index.html)
reported a Contraloría note on 2013,
[ProActivo](https://proactivo.com.pe/universidades-como-aumentar-ejecucion-del-dinero-del-canon/)
an El Comercio count of 2014, and
[Gestión](https://gestion.pe/economia/snmpe-universidades-nacionales-recibieron-s-498-millones-de-canon-y-regalias-este-ano-rmmn-noticia/)
the mining society SNMPE's counts of 2012 to 2022.

- From 2012 to 2025 universities received S/ 6,554 million of canon and
  royalties, most of it mining canon, and spent S/ 4,867 million.
- They spent 66.2% of their canon budget in 2025, S/ 739 million of
  S/ 1,117 million. They spent 13.0% in 2012 and passed half for the first
  time in 2019.
- They began 2026 with S/ 2,885 million unspent, ten times the S/ 283 million
  they began 2012 with. Their 2026 budget includes S/ 544 million of it.
- 79.8% of their spending bought buildings, equipment and other assets.
  Buildings took 43.6% and machinery and equipment 20.7%. MEF classes 8.8%
  under applied and basic research.
- The figures the press printed for 2013, 2014, 2021 and 2022 agree with
  MEF's records.

The figures come from the budget and revenue records of the Ministerio de
Economía y Finanzas (MEF). They show what universities received, budgeted,
spent and bought, not whether the spending followed the rule in force each
year. None of the reports cited alleges misuse of funds.

## Received

![Canon received and spent by public universities](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/received.svg)

MEF's revenue records show the canon and royalties sent to each university.
Transfers were S/ 429 million in 2012, peaked at S/ 855 million in 2022 and
were S/ 653 million in 2025. Spending exceeded the year's transfers only in
2019, 2020 and 2025. 41 universities had a canon budget in 2012, 54 in 2025
and 63 in 2026.

![Where universities' canon comes from](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/resources.svg)

Mining canon is the largest source, S/ 2,730 million of the S/ 6,554 million
received from 2012 to 2025. Gas canon and FOCAM, the fund for the regions of
the Camisea gas field, follow with S/ 1,972 million, then mining royalties
with S/ 978 million and oil canon with S/ 549 million.

## Budgeted and spent

![Public universities' canon budget and spending](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/canon.svg)

The opening budget (PIA) is the one in the budget law, which Congress passes
by 30 November for the next year. The universities' canon PIA was
S/ 355 million in 2012, S/ 916 million in 2025 and S/ 890 million in 2026.
The budget after changes (PIM) adds the year's changes, chiefly the balance a
university did not spend the year before. It ran above the PIA every year
except 2024 and reached S/ 1,117 million in 2025. Because each PIM carries
earlier balances, the yearly PIMs cannot be added up as money received.
Spending accrued (devengado) grew from S/ 69 million in 2012 to
S/ 739 million in 2025. 2026 runs to the date of the data.

![How much of their canon universities spend](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/execution.svg)

![How much of their 2025 canon universities spent](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/universities.svg)

The Universidad Nacional de San Antonio Abad del Cusco has the largest canon
budget: S/ 162.9 million in 2025, of which it spent 60.1%. In 2014 it had
S/ 154.8 million and spent 17.0%. That year the Universidad Nacional del
Santa had S/ 18.3 million and spent S/ 13,774. The Universidad Nacional de
Arte Diego Quispe Tito del Cusco spent 3.4% of S/ 30.4 million in 2025. San
Marcos gets almost no canon: S/ 92,567 in 2014.
[`numbers.json`](numbers.json) lists every university.

## Unspent

![Canon universities hold unspent](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/balance.svg)

Canon a university does not spend stays with it and enters the next year as
a balance. The balance rose every year except 2020. A university can spend
only what its budget includes, and the 2026 budget includes 19% of the
balance. The records add up: the balance at the start of 2025, plus that
year's income, less its spending, gives S/ 2,891 million, and MEF records
S/ 2,885 million at the start of 2026.

![Which universities hold the most unspent canon](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/balances.svg)

The Universidad Nacional Intercultural de Quillabamba holds S/ 664 million,
23% of the total, and budgeted S/ 11.5 million of it for 2026. The
Universidad Nacional de Arte Diego Quispe Tito del Cusco holds
S/ 338 million, Santiago Antúnez de Mayolo S/ 274 million, and San Antonio
Abad and Del Santa S/ 254 million each.

## What the money bought

![What universities spend their canon on](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/uses.svg)

![What universities buy with their canon](https://research.rising.sh/peru-universities-canon/items.svg)

MEF codes each line twice: by functional group, the purpose, and by spending
item, the thing bought. From 2012 to 2025, university education took 46.3%
of canon spending, infrastructure and equipment 41.8%, applied research 7.2%
and basic research 1.6%. Projects took 69.4%, and the largest are new
faculties and campus buildings. The largest of all is San Antonio Abad's
project for teaching, research and extension support, S/ 126.6 million.

Regional governments also passed canon to universities as transfers. MEF
books those under donations and transfers, not under the canon, so the
charts leave them out. Their spending peaked at S/ 116.0 million in 2018, and
the last were in 2022.

## The press figures

- **Contraloría, 2013**, reported by El Búho: its S/ 828.5 million available
  is MEF's PIM, S/ 828.8 million. MEF's year-end spending gives 20.5% spent,
  where El Búho printed 14.1%.
- **El Comercio, 2014**, reported by ProActivo: its S/ 771 million and
  S/ 216.2 million match MEF's PIM and spending to S/ 0.3 million and
  S/ 0.1 million. Those two figures give 28.0%, where it printed 28.5%.
- **SNMPE**, reported by Gestión: its S/ 306 million for 2021 and
  S/ 498 million for January to September 2022 match MEF, and so do the
  canon and royalty parts and each of the ten universities it named. Its
  S/ 2,072 million for 2012 to 2021 is 2.7% above the S/ 2,018 million of
  mining canon and royalties MEF recorded; the gap is not explained. With
  gas, oil and other canon, universities received S/ 3,777 million in those
  years. Its 39% spent is ten years of spending over ten years of PIM summed,
  39.3%, a sum that counts each balance again in every year it is carried
  over.

## Limits

- **Current soles.** Amounts are not adjusted for inflation.
- **Classification, not content.** MEF's functional group says what a line
  is for. A building coded under university education may hold
  laboratories, and a research line may pay for equipment.
- **Accrual.** Spending accrued (devengado) counts goods received and work
  done. Money committed but not yet accrued is not counted as spent.
- **Received.** Received is what MEF's revenue records book as collected
  under transfers. The balance carried over is booked as collected in the
  year it enters and is counted apart. The canon rubro's other income,
  S/ 131 million in 2025, is not counted as received.
- **Rules.** The article does not trace the use rules that budget laws after
  2009 set, so it does not test spending against them.
- **Universities.** The article counts every pliego from 510 to 583 in the
  education sector, which are the public universities, except 540, the
  Asamblea Nacional de Rectores that SUNEDU replaced in 2014.
- **2026.** The PIM and spending of 2026 change until the year closes.

## Data

MEF's open data portal publishes the
[comparison of budget and spending](https://datosabiertos.mef.gob.pe) as three
zips of five years each, 2012 to 2026, and one daily file per year from 2025.
The 2022 to 2026 zip repeats each row's 2025 figures in its 2026 columns, so
2026 comes from the daily file. The canon is the funding rubro "Canon y
sobrecanon, regalías, renta de aduanas y participaciones", which also holds
royalties.

Revenue for 2012 to 2024 comes from MEF's
[Consulta Amigable de Ingresos](https://apps5.mineco.gob.pe/transparenciaingresos/Navegador/default.aspx),
by university, revenue group and resource type, and by month for January to
September 2022, the months the SNMPE counted. From 2025 it comes from the
open data's daily revenue file.

## Reproduce

[`peru_universities_canon.py`](peru_universities_canon.py) charts the
committed tables: [`canon.csv`](canon.csv) by year, university, project
type, spending item and functional group, [`projects.csv`](projects.csv) by
project, and the names in [`items.csv`](items.csv),
[`project_names.csv`](project_names.csv) and
[`universities.csv`](universities.csv), and revenue in
[`revenue.csv`](revenue.csv) and
[`revenue_2022_monthly.csv`](revenue_2022_monthly.csv). `--fetch` rebuilds
them from MEF's open data; it streams about 5 GB and takes ten minutes. It
keeps the revenue of 2012 to 2024, which was pulled once from Consulta
Amigable de Ingresos in a browser as
[`docs/mef.md`](../../docs/mef.md) describes. The rerun workflow fetches each
Monday.

```bash
mise run install
uv run studies/peru-universities-canon/peru_universities_canon.py --fetch
```
